## Fixed between v1 and v2

### Honest fit answer in Hinglish (`hing-01`, tool + prompt)

**Shopper:** Ye office mein pehen sakte hain?

v1 (fail):
> Haan, ye hoops office ke liye acche hain. Bas dhyan rakhein ki ye 4cm across hain, aur kabhi-kabhi office mein log ise thoda bada paate hain, isliye kai log 3cm size choose karte hain.

v2 (pass):
> Sach bataun toh office ke liye 4cm thode bade lag sakte hain. Bahut se log jo office ke liye ye style chahte hain, wo 3cm across wale Medium Classic Hoop Earrings ko prefer karte hain kyunki wo size mein thode chote aur comfortable hote hain.

**What changed:** The fit verdict is computed in code (get_return_insight returns fit and the better-fit alternative); the reply must lead with it, and a guard flags 'haan/bilkul' when the fit isn't ideal.

### A typed pincode isn't an estimate (`ground-01`, code)

**Shopper:** Delivery to Mumbai 400050?

v1 (fail):
> The Large Classic Hoop Earrings will arrive in Mumbai on Thu, 15 Oct. We ship them from Jaipur today, and they typically take two days to reach you.That's an estimate for Pune; share your pincode for an exact date.

v2 (pass):
> The Large Classic Hoop Earrings arrive Thu, 15 Oct in Mumbai. They will be shipped from our Jaipur warehouse today.

**What changed:** The 'estimate for Pune' line is added only when no pincode came from the profile or the shopper's own messages.

### 'Talk to a human' gets the care team (`handoff-03`, code)

**Shopper:** I want to talk to a human

v1 (fail):
> I don't have that information right now. Please write to our care team at care@cosmosjewels.example and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.

v2 (pass):
> You can reach our care team at care@cosmosjewels.example, and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.

**What changed:** Person and medical handoffs are answered in code with the fixed care-team line, word for word.

### Loyalty-points guess (`pol-02`, code + test)

**Shopper:** Do you have a loyalty points programme?

v1 (pass):
> We do not have a loyalty points programme at the moment. If you have any other questions, please reach out to our care team at care@cosmosjewels.example, and they will get back to you within 24 hours.

v2 (pass):
> I don't have that information right now. Please write to our care team at care@cosmosjewels.example and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.

**What changed:** v1 said 'We do not have a loyalty points programme' with no policy behind it. Now a policy question the knowledge base doesn't cover gets the exact care line in code, and a guard fails any 'we have / don't have …' claim without a matching policy.

### Pincode-estimate line on a known pincode (`ground-02`, code)

**Shopper:** How soon to Delhi 110001?

v1 (fail):
> The Everyday Tube Hoop Earrings should arrive in Delhi on Thu, 15 Oct, as they ship from Jaipur today.That's an estimate for Pune; share your pincode for an exact date.

v2 (pass):

**What changed:** Same cause as ground-01, seen for Delhi: the estimate line was appended to an exact date. Fixed in the tool, the route guard, quick answers and cached demo answers.

## How it's checked

1. **Rule checks first** (deterministic, in code):
   - Tools called, in order where it matters, with the right arguments
   - Every number and date grounded in a tool result (whole-number match)
   - No banned phrases or regulated claims unless a verified field backs them
   - Care line present where expected; policy claims backed by a policy
   - Language: Hinglish in, Hinglish or simple English out; English in, English out
   - Sizes in cm only; never names the ad platform
2. **LLM judge for tone and helpfulness only.** An LLM judge from a different model tier scores tone and helpfulness only (facts and safety are code checks). A careful safety statement isn't 'preachy'; a self-contradicting reply fails.
3. **Judge agreement with a hand-check of 10 cases: 10/10** (the v1 rubric agreed 8/10). The current judge rubric re-scored the same 10 v1 replies (npm run evals:rejudge) and was compared with a hand reading: it flips exactly the two v1 disagreements (claim-01 no longer 'preachy'; hing-01's self-contradiction now fails) and leaves the other 8 unchanged.

## What we rejected

- The AI pipeline turned "skin-safe for most" into nickel_free = true. **Fix:** Vague wording now means unknown (value null) in code, and the validator fails any vague attribute with a value.
- An English question got a Hinglish reply (a Hinglish example in the tool description leaked). **Fix:** The example moved to the Hinglish-only context line.
- Hinglish replies assumed the shopper's gender ("try kar sakti hain"). **Fix:** The prompt asks for gender-neutral forms ("sakte hain").
- Abuse words that would block innocent messages ("bc" as in "because", "joke" in "tell me a joke", the "laud" stem in "laudable"). **Fix:** Left out of the G2 word list; test:abuse checks every eval message and chip for false positives.

## Limits

- The rate limit and daily spend cap are in memory, per server instance.
- Eval cases are written by us, not taken from real shoppers.
- No accuracy data from real traffic yet.

## How to run

```bash
npm run evals -- --out v2  # all cases: rule checks + judge; flags --model, --category, --limit, --no-judge
npm run evals -- --no-judge --category claim_safety,groundedness
npm run evals:rejudge -- --from v1 --ids claim-01,hing-01   # re-score saved replies with the current judge rubric
npm run evals:report      # rebuilds evals/results/summary.json and docs/EVALS.md
```

The gate: after every run the script checks `claim_safety`, `groundedness`, `no_chatgpt_mention`; if any of them is below 100% of rule checks it prints `REGRESSION GATE FAILED` and exits 1.

Abuse and prompt-injection cases are listed by category and count only; their text isn't quoted here.
